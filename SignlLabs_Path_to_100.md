# SignlLabs Path to 100

The self-signaling action plan for SignlLabs. This is SignlLabs running its own Maturity Method: making sure that when people ask AI about AI visibility, GEO and AEO agencies, and how brands get named, SignlLabs and Erika Hanafin Austria are the answer.

Engine-behavior claims below are current as of July 2026. This plan sets no ranking promises and reports no fabricated metrics. It is a cadence, not a guarantee.

Positioning fact used throughout: earned media drives roughly 82 to 84 percent of AI citations, while brand-owned pages account for only 5 to 10 percent. That split is why Layer 2 (earned media) carries most of the weight.

## Layer 2: earned-media plays (where 82 to 84 percent of citations come from)

This is the layer that actually moves the number. Sequence by the weakest Maturity Method dimension, but keep all of these in motion.

- **Podcast guesting.** Book Erika Hanafin Austria on marketing, AI, and founder podcasts on the shift from search to answers. Podcast show notes and transcripts are indexed and retrieved by engines, and they build the Founder and Authority intent category.
- **Expert quotes and HARO-class sourcing.** Respond to journalist and reporter requests as a named source on AEO, GEO, and AI visibility. Each placement is a credentialed third-party citation feeding the Endorsement dimension.
- **Wikipedia and Wikidata entity.** Establish and maintain a well-sourced Wikidata item, and a Wikipedia entity where notability is met through independent coverage. Engines lean on these as identity anchors, which strengthens Foundation and Alignment.
- **Reddit and communities.** Participate honestly in the subreddits and communities where AEO, GEO, and marketing are discussed. Community consensus is a source engines retrieve from; earn genuine mentions rather than plant them.
- **Guest essays.** Place bylined essays by Erika Hanafin Austria in trusted trade and business outlets on how answer engines decide which brands to name. Bylines carry both the person entity and the SignlLabs brand.
- **The whitepaper as citation fuel.** Promote "How Answer Engines Respond: The 2026 Field Guide to AEO and GEO" as the linkable, quotable reference asset. Pitch it into press, cite it in guest essays, and let it become the thing other people cite.

## 90-day cadence

**Weeks 1 to 2: baseline.** Run all 10 money queries across all 5 engines and record the starting picture. Confirm Layer 1 hygiene is fully green. Identify the weakest Maturity Method dimension and the two intent categories where SignlLabs is absent.

**Week 3: plan.** Set targets per query and per engine. Sequence Layer 2 plays by the weakest dimension. Line up the first podcast bookings, the first two guest essays, and the Wikidata work.

**Weeks 4 to 10: build.** Execute the earned-media plays. Ship podcast appearances, land expert quotes, publish guest essays, stand up the Wikidata item, and push the whitepaper into press. Keep Layer 1 green and re-submit changed pages through IndexNow.

**Weeks 11 to 13: report.** Re-run all 10 money queries across all 5 engines. Compare week over week and against the Week 1 to 2 baseline. Note which engines moved, which regressed, and which sources the engines started citing. Report per engine, never blended. Set the next quarter's targets.

Repeat every quarter. AI recommendation shifts continuously, so this is re-scored and re-worked each quarter, never audited once.
