What is AI signaling

Not SEO. Not PR. A new discipline.

AI signaling is the practice of engineering how AI understands, trusts, and recommends your brand. The engines do not rank pages. They synthesize an answer and name a few brands. Whether you are one of them is decided by signals most teams are not managing.

Top Google links and AI-cited sources now overlap less than twenty percent of the time. Different inputs, a different game. The brands that get named are not the loudest. They are the best signaled.

SignlLabs is the agency-led practice that pairs the methodology with the execution. Dashboards tell you the score. We tell you the move, then we make it.

Not SEO

Keywords and backlinks optimize for a results page. The answer engines read source credibility and factual consensus instead.

Not PR

Old PR chased impressions and column inches. The engines reward being the trusted, cited source, not the loudest voice.

Engineered authority

Built deliberately in the sources the models read, then measured, then moved, quarter over quarter.

The mechanism

Why the signal compounds instead of resetting.

This is the part that is hard to see from outside. An answer engine does not read your website and decide. It reads what the rest of the internet says about you, and then it decides. So the work is not producing more of your own voice. It is changing what the engine finds when it goes looking.

  1. 1

    Measure what the engines retrieve

    We put the ten buyer questions to all five engines and record more than whether you were named — we record what each one cited when it answered.

  2. 2

    Find the source gap

    Those citations reveal which sources each engine trusts for your category. Your absence from them is the gap, and it is specific: named publications, named platforms, named records — not a general instruction to do more PR.

  3. 3

    Get on the record where they look

    Earned citation, entity records and structured answers placed into exactly those sources. The engines re-crawl and re-retrieve on their own schedule, which is why this is a quarterly discipline and not a launch.

  4. 4

    Re-score and see what took

    Next quarter the same questions show which placements the engines picked up and which they ignored. That is evidence about how this category’s engines behave, and almost nobody is collecting it.

  5. 5

    Aim the next wave with it

    Each cycle narrows the guesswork. Wave four is aimed better than wave one because three waves of evidence tell you where these engines actually look for this category.

That is the compounding: not more volume every quarter — better aim every quarter. And because the sources stay on the record, wave one keeps paying in wave six.

SEO vs AEO vs AI Signaling

Three disciplines. Only one wins the recommendation.

They sound similar and they are not. Each targets a different moment, and AI Signaling is the only one built for how the answer engine actually decides.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Earns a ranking on a page of links.

Optimizes keywords and backlinks so a human clicks your result. Built for the ten blue links, a page most buyers now skip.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Earns the snippet an AI reads back.

Structures your content into clean, liftable answers so a model can quote you. Necessary hygiene, but it only makes you eligible.

The SignlLabs discipline

AI Signaling

Earns the recommendation itself.

Engineers the earned, third-party authority the engines trust, so when someone asks AI who is best, you are named. This is where the decision is actually made.

Why keywords cannot win it: an AI does not answer the question it is asked. It quietly splits one prompt into roughly five hidden research queries, then names the brands it trusts across all of them. You cannot keyword your way into that answer. You earn it, across every source the engine reads.

Answers

AI signaling, answered.

Short, source-ready answers to the questions buyers and journalists actually ask AI about our category.

Do you guarantee we will be named first?

No — and anyone who does is selling something they cannot deliver. Nobody controls what ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot or Perplexity say, and the engines change their retrieval and their models without notice. What we commit to is the instrument and the work: a measured baseline you can audit, a plan tied to your weakest dimensions, the execution to put your brand on the record in the sources those engines retrieve from, and an honest per-engine re-score every quarter showing what moved and what did not. Brands that run this consistently get named more often. That is a pattern we can show you, not a promise we can make.

What is AI signaling?

AI signaling is the practice of engineering how AI understands, trusts, and recommends your brand. The engines do not rank pages, they synthesize an answer and name a few brands. AI signaling is the work of becoming one of the names.

How is AI signaling different from SEO?

SEO optimizes for a page of blue links. AI signaling optimizes for the answer itself. The overlap is under twenty percent, because engines weight source credibility and third-party consensus, not keywords and backlinks.

Can a brand control what AI says about it?

Not directly. You cannot inform the model from your own website alone. Brand-owned content is only five to ten percent of what AI cites. The dominant lever is earned and paid media in the sources engines already trust.

What is the Maturity Method?

The Maturity Method is a zero to one hundred methodology that scores how recommendable a brand is to AI across six dimensions and ten intent categories, measured on all five major engines.

Which AI engines does SignlLabs measure?

SignlLabs scores your brand across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Copilot, in both base and search modes, and reports each engine separately rather than blending them.

How do you get recommended by ChatGPT and other AI?

Fix the owned foundation with schema and clear answer blocks, then earn citations in the third-party sources AI retrieves from: review platforms, communities, and trusted press. SignlLabs measures the gap and engineers the lift every quarter.
