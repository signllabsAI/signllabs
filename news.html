Practice launch · August 31, 2026

GCW Launches SignlLabs™, a Proprietary AI Signaling Practice Built to Earn Brand Visibility Across Artificial Intelligence Platforms

New practice gives clients a measurable path to being recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Perplexity, built for AI rather than retrofitted from SEO.

NEW YORK, N.Y. — Global Communication Works (GCW), the strategic public relations and communications agency, today announced the formation of SignlLabs™, its proprietary AI Signaling practice. SignlLabs gives GCW clients a dedicated methodology for earning visibility and recommendations across the artificial intelligence platforms consumers now turn to for answers.

“For nearly 40 years, GCW has built our clients’ reputations one relationship, one story and one placement at a time. SignlLabs is the natural next chapter. Consumers no longer stop at a search engine. They are asking AI platforms for recommendations, and brands need a real strategy to show up there, not a retrofit of old SEO tactics. That is exactly what we built.”Suzy Ginsburg, Founder and CEO, GCW

Search engine optimization earns a ranking on a page of links. Answer engine optimization earns the snippet an AI reads back. AI Signaling earns the recommendation itself. The distinction matters because an answer engine does not simply answer the question it is asked. It quietly splits a single prompt into several hidden research queries, then composes an answer from the sources it trusts most, sources that are overwhelmingly earned through third-party endorsement rather than a brand’s own website.

SignlLabs pairs GCW’s earned media strategy with a dedicated AI recommendation framework, measuring performance across the five platforms most consumers now rely on for answers: ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity.

GCW now measures success across both traditional and AI channels, tracking impressions, reach and engagement alongside AI recommendation lift, giving clients a full view of how a campaign performs wherever audiences get their news and their answers.

SignlLabs is already embedded in many GCW campaigns across the agency’s women’s health, consumer health and lifestyle client roster, and the practice will be central to new business pursuits going forward.

“SEO is about being the answer. AI Signaling is about being the recommendation. When someone asks an answer engine which brand is best, the platform runs its own research behind the scenes and names the brands it trusts. That trust lives in earned media, not on a brand’s homepage.”Erika Austria, lead of SignlLabs

Media contact: Brad Ginsburg, Global Communication Works, brad@gcw.agency. More at signllabs.ai and gcw.agency.

Licensing · August 31, 2026

Global Communication Works Opens SignlLabs™, Its Proprietary AI Signaling Practice, for License to PR Firms, Ad Agencies, and Web Developers

Licensees gain a ready-built methodology for earning client visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Perplexity, without building an AI Signaling practice from scratch.

NEW YORK, N.Y. — Global Communication Works (GCW), the strategic public relations and communications agency, today announced that SignlLabs, its proprietary AI Signaling practice, is now available for license to public relations firms, advertising agencies, and web development companies. The license enables agencies and developers to offer their clients a measurable AI Signaling program without having to build the methodology in-house.

“SignlLabs was built inside GCW, but it does not need to stay inside GCW. Any agency or developer, whether it specializes in public relations, advertising or web development, can now license SignlLabs and give its own clients a real path to AI visibility instead of trying to reverse-engineer one. This is not about protecting a competitive advantage. It is about setting the standard the whole industry can build on.”Brad Ginsburg, Partner and President, GCW

SignlLabs measures a brand’s presence and recommendation strength across the five platforms most consumers now turn to for answers: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Perplexity. Built from the ground up rather than adapted from existing search practices, the methodology has been embedded across GCW’s client campaigns before being opened to outside licensees.

Licensees gain access to the SignlLabs framework, measurement approach and training needed to run AI Signaling programs for their own client rosters, spanning industries from consumer health to retail. GCW will continue to support licensees as the AI platforms themselves evolve, keeping the methodology current as recommendation algorithms change.

The license is available to public relations firms, advertising agencies and web development companies of any size, and interested parties can reach GCW directly to discuss terms.

Growth advisory · August 31, 2026

Global Communication Works Expands SignlLabs™ into a Strategic Growth Advisory Practice

Hands-on counsel on where to invest, how to compete, and where growth comes next, for companies from the Fortune 100 to early-stage startups.

NEW YORK, N.Y. — Global Communication Works (GCW) today announced that SignlLabs™, its proprietary AI Signaling practice, is expanding beyond visibility and measurement into strategic growth advisory. The new capability brings hands-on counsel on where to invest, how to compete, and where growth will come next for companies of every scale, from the Fortune 100 to early-stage startups.

“Companies do not just need to know how AI sees them. They need a roadmap for what to do about it. That is the gap we are closing. SignlLabs is bringing consulting-caliber strategic thinking and a clear, phased path to execution to a discipline that, until now, has mostly lived with top consulting companies.”Erika Austria, lead of SignlLabs

SignlLabs begins with deep research and development: sizing the market opportunity through a total addressable market and serviceable addressable market view, distilling both into a directional summary of the size of the prize, mapping where the unmet opportunities lie, and benchmarking precedent comparables for what the market already pays. From there, SignlLabs advises on how to build on that opportunity, identifying where the growth lies and developing the roadmap to capture it.

The approach is already at work. For an emerging brand, SignlLabs mapped an entire category from a blank slate. Nothing like it existed yet, not as a brand, not as a digital destination, not as a trusted source consumers could turn to. The market sizing alone pointed to a roughly $36 billion total addressable market with $16 billion serviceable in the U.S., directional, and SignlLabs used that opportunity to hand the brand a complete go-to-market plan: a redesigned website built to be the trusted, cited answer across every major AI platform and large language model, and a consumer app designed to build community, deliver education and establish the brand as the name consumers and retailers trust most.

The expanded offering positions SignlLabs as a growth partner as well as a measurement platform, helping clients act on AI Signaling insights with the same discipline GCW brings to earned media, brand positioning and reputation management. The scope of the engagement, whether audit only, full build, or ongoing monitoring, is shaped to the size and stage of the client.
